The Questions We Asked

The quotations displayed below come from responses to two open-ended questions in the Palm Cove community survey. Respondents were not specifically asked about walkability, traffic, parking or pedestrianisation. These themes emerged naturally through their answers.

Question 3
“Thinking about how Palm Cove looks, feels and functions today, what would you most hate to lose?”
Question 4
“Looking ahead, what should we fix — and what should we add — to keep Palm Cove, Palm Cove?”
