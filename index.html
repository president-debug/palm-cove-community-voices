Palm Cove Community Survey

Walkability

Residents repeatedly identified walking, footpaths, boardwalks, accessibility, cycle connections and pedestrian comfort as key elements of Palm Cove's identity.

Village Character

Responses consistently valued a calm, low-rise village atmosphere, natural beauty, shade trees and a relaxed pace of life.

Traffic & Parking

Many responses linked reducing traffic, relocating parking, and improving the pedestrian environment to protecting Palm Cove's future.
